Garena Youth Championship (GYC) is part of the Garena Goes to School program, which is a positive esports empowerment program among students in  Indonesia. In addition, GYC is a forum to attract talented students at the school level and produce national champions at the student level.
The Garena Youth Championship 2025 is supported by the Indonesian Esports Executive Board, the Ministry of Youth and Sports and the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology. This time, it is presented with a total prizepool of Rp275,000,000 IDR across all tournament stages with the slogan; "Become FF Champion and Proud of Your School".

Format
City Qualifiers: August 4th - 31st, 2025
82 cities.
Top 142 teams advance to Regional Qualifiers.

Online Qualifiers: September 16th - 27th, 2025
Maximum 500 teams.
Top 2 teams advance to Regional Qualifiers.

Regional Qualifiers: September 30th - October 23rd, 2025
144 teams across 12 regions.
Winner of each region qualify for Grand Finals.

Grand Finals: November 29th, 2025 at  Jakarta
12 teams.
