Free Fire World Series - Vietnam is the official Free Fire regional tournament held in  Vietnam, seeding into the regional championship.


About
Day 2 and 3 of the Group Stage were played off-stream due to the State Funeral of the General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam Nguyễn Phú Trọng.. The matches were then rebroadcasted on August 27th.

Format
*Registrations from June 24th - July 3rd, 2024

Qualifier: July 6th - 7th, 2024
For detailed Qualifier format, Click here.

Group Stage: July 19th - 21st, 2024
18 qualified teams are split into 3 groups of 6 teams
18 matches across 3 matchdays (6 matches each day).
Top 6 teams advance to Grand Finals.
Grand Finals: July 27th - 28th, 2024 at Free Fire Studio,  Ho Chi Minh City
12 teams:
1 Vietnamese team relegated from FFWS Southeast Asia 2024 Spring
2 teams from FFWS Vietnam 2024 Spring
3 teams from Clan War
6 teams from Group Stage
12 matches (6 each day).
