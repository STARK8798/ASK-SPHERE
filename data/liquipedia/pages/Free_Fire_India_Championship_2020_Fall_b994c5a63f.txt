Format
FFC Mode Qualifiers: August 13th - August 25th, 2020   Online.
Best 5 of 8 matches will be considered and points are awarded accordingly
Top 48 Teams will be split into 4 groups of 12, Top 3 in each group proceed to League Circuit
4th-6th in each group enter LCQ, Top 6 proceed to League Circuit
League Circuit: September 9th - 25th, 2020   Online.
Circuit Points are distributed based on placement within the same group in a matchday
20-17-14-10-7-4
9 Days of League Play - 18 Teams are split into three groups of 6 teams, Triple Round Robin
Top 2 of each group qualified for Grand Finals
Bottom 4 in each group entered Play-Ins
Play-Ins: October 2nd, 2020   Online.
Top 6 qualified for Grand Finals
Grand Finals: October 18th, 2020   Online.
Champions qualify for FFCS Asia Finals
2nd & 3rd qualify for FFCS Asia Play-Ins
Top 6 qualify for FFIC 2021 Spring
7th - 12th proceed to FFIC 2021 Spring - Closed Qualifiers
