Free Fire Liga de Ascenso (abbreviated to FFLA); translated to Free Fire Ascension League, is a regional Free Fire league across  Latin America, organized by Garena and Liga Prosur.
The league acts as the ascension league for LATAM teams to the FFWS LATAM 2026 - Promotion Series.


About


Format
*Registrations from Nov 27th - Dec 4th, 2025.
Round of 16: December 9th  2025
96 teams, divided into 8 groups of 12.
Each group plays 3 matches.
Top 6 teams each group advance to Quarterfinal.

Quarterfinal: December 11th, 2025
48 teams, divided into 4 groups of 12.
Each group plays 3 matches.
Top 6 teams each group advance to Semifinal.

Semifinal: December 13th, 2025
24 teams, divided into 2 groups of 12.
Each group plays 4 matches.
Top 12 teams advance to Final.

Final: December 17th, 2025
12 teams.
Champion Rush format is applied. Click here for details about Champion Rush format


Allocations
Winner qualify for  Free Fire World Series - Latin America 2026: Promotion Series
2nd - 4th placed teams advance to  Free Fire Liga de Ascenso 2026 - Finals
