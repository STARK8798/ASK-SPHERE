Clan War (Vietnamese: Đại Chiến Quân Đoàn) is a semi-pro tournament for clans in the Vietnam server, run by Garena Vietnam.
The 2025 edition is the 11th season of the tournament.


About
November 28th - Due to impersonation,  Hung Nguyen 2 has been disqualified and banned for 12 months.  GEM Esports, the next highest-ranked team, has replaced its.


Format
* Registrations from November 1st- 9th, 2025
Provinces Qualifier: November 16th, 2025
The provinces compete offline at locations designated by the organizers.
Provinces Group Stage: 3 matches.
Provinces Finals: 3 matches.
Top 3 clans from Hanoi, HCM City, Hue, Da Nang advance to National Qualifier.
Top 2 clans from remaining provinces advance to National Qualifier.
National Qualifier: November 23rd, 2025
72 clans, divided into 6 groups of 12.
Top 3 clans each group advance to Group Stage.
Group Stage: December 5th - 14th, 2025
18 clans, divided into 3 groups of 12.
36 matches across 6 matchdays, each clan plays total 24 matches.
Top 12 clans qualify for Grand Finals.
Grand Finals: December 28th, 2025
12 clans.
6 matches.


Allocations
Top 12 clans qualify for  Vietnam League Season 1
