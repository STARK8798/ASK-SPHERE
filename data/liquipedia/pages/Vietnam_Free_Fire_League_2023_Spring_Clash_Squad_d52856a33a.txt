Vietnam Free Fire League is the official Free Fire League held in  Vietnam.
The 2023 Spring season is the fourth season that brings together the 12 best teams in the country. For the first time ever, the Clash Squad will officially be included in a professional league.Unlike other Clash Squad tournaments, VFL Clash Squad is an entirely separate league, even from the VFL Battle Royale, although teams can still fill the Battle Royale roster to Clash Squad league.


About


Qualification
12 teams:
Top 7 teams from Closed Qualifiers
5 teams directly invited


Format
Group Stage: March 24th - April 2nd, 2023 in FF Studio, Hồ Chí Minh City.
12 teams are split into 3 groups of 4 teams
Double Round Robin
All matches are played in a Bo3, with all games are played in a Bo7
Each game win grants 1 point
Top 2 teams of each group and the best 2 third-placed teams among all groups advance to Playoffs.

Playoffs: 
Single Elimination Bracket
Quarterfinals: April 8th - 9th, 2023 in FF Studio, Hồ Chí Minh City.
Semifinals: April 15th - 16th, 2023 in FF Studio, Hồ Chí Minh City.
Grand Finals: April 23rd, 2023 in FF Studio, Hồ Chí Minh City.
Quarterfinals matches are played in a Bo7, with all games are played in a Bo7
Semifinals matches and Grand Finals are played in a Bo9, with all games are played in a Bo7


Allocations
 VFL Clash Squad:
Top 4 teams secured spots to VFL Summer 2023 - Clash Squad
 Clash Squad SEA Cup:
Top 4 teams qualify for Clash Squad SEA Cup 2023
