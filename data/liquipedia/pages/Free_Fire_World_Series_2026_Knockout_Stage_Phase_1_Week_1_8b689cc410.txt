Format
Knockout Stage - Phase 1: November 6th - 22nd, 2026
24 teams.
The stage is divided into two (2) phases:
Phase 1 (Week 1 - 2): November 6th - 15th, 2026
Teams are divided into 4 groups of 6.
2 weeks, matchdays every Friday to Sunday.
36 matches in total across 6 matchdays.
Teams play in a round-robin format.
Each team play total of 24 matches.
Based on the teams' final placements, Top 18 teams are divided into 4 pools for Phase 2:
Pool 1 - Top 12
Pool 2 - 13th - 14th
Pool 3 - 15th - 16th
Pool 4 - 17th - 18th
19th-20th placed teams relegate to Last Chance.
Remaining teams are eliminated.
