EVOS Fams Cup is an Online Tournament that has been regularly held by EVOS since 2020 in the Free Fire and Mobile Legend Bang Bang game categories. The winner of the EVOS Fams Cup also has the opportunity to be invited to the EVOS Fams Cup Pro Series and has the opportunity to become an EVOS Pro Player.
