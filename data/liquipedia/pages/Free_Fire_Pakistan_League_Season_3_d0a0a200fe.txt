Free Fire Pakistan League is the main Free Fire league in  Pakistan, bringing together the best 18 teams of the country.


About


Broadcast Talent
Producer: Bloodline (Danish Ayub)
Commentators: Dowdy (Muhammad Ali Dowdy) Mak (Mohtashim Mak) Sallu (Sahibzada Muhammad Suleman Afridi)


Format
Group Stage: February 19th - March 13rd, 2022 in Garena Studio, Karachi.
18 teams:
Top 6 from Season 2
Top 12 from Play-Offs
4 Weeks of League Play, each with 2 Days
Round Robin Format: Teams are split into three groups of 6 teams
Each team plays 36 matches in total in the league, every Saturday and Sunday
Top 12 advance to Grand Finals
Ban-Pic Map Pools system is used in the event
