Free Fire World Series - Latin America (abbreviated as FFWS LATAM) is the inaugural regional league for  Latin America organized by Garena, replacing the Free Fire League Latinoamerica.
Split 1 represents the first half of the championship, also known as the EWC Qualifier.


About
 Janus T2 was disqualified from participating in the Split Finals and overall league for failing to deliver their players' no-debt letters before the due date as depicted in the tournament rulebook. As a result,  Furious Gaming replaces  Janus T2 in the Finals.
After  Janus T2 submitted the required documents, their disqualification from the league was lifted. However,  Furious Gaming will continue to replace them in the Split Finals.

Format
Regular Phase: March  22nd - May 19th, 2024
Definition Phase: Mar 22nd - Apr 7th
18 teams, divided into 3 groups of 6.
3 weeks, matchdays every Friday to Sunday.
54 matches in total, teams play 36 matches in total.
Group Phase: Apr 27th - May 19th
18 teams, divided into 3 groups of 6. The best teams in Definition Phase can choose their group rivals.
4 weeks, matchdays every Saturday to Sunday (except the last week has 3 matchdays)
54 matches in total, teams play 36 matches in total.
Top 12 teams advance to Grand Finals.
The best team after each week get 6 headstart points for Finals.

Grand Finals: June 1st, 2024
12 teams.
8 matches.
