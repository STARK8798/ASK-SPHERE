Free Fire World Series - Bangladesh (abbreviated FFWS BD) is the official Free Fire league in  Bangladesh.


About
May 25th -  Aerox is disqualified from the tournament because of cheating during the Knockout Stage. Following this disqualification,  Reflex United (13th position in Knockout Stage) advances to the Grand Finals instead.
June 6th - The Grand Finals which was originally scheduled to be held offline is held online due to unforeseen circumstances.


Format
In-Game Qualifier: March 20th - 21st, 2026
15 matches, the best 5 matches are counted.
Top 30 teams advance to League Stage.

League Stage: April 30th - May 16th, 2026
Round of 36: April 30th - May 9th, 2026
36 teams:
30 teams from In-Game Qualifier.
6 teams from  FFBPL Season 2.
Teams are divided into 6 groups of 6.
180 matches in total across 5 matchdays.
Teams play in a round-robin format.
Each team play total of 30 matches.
Top 24 teams advance to Round of 24.
Round of 24: May 14th - May 16th, 2026
24 teams, divided into 4 groups of 6.
72 matches in total across 3 matchdays.
Teams play in a round-robin format.
Each team play total of 18 matches.
Top 18 teams advance to Knockout Stage.
Knockout Stage: May 21st - May 23rd, 2026
18 teams, divided into 3 groups of 6.
18 matches in total across 3 matchdays.
Teams play in a round-robin format.
Each team play total of 12 matches.
Top 12 teams advance to Grand Finals.

Grand Finals: June 6th, 2026 at ICCB Hall 4 (Noboratri),  Dhaka
12 teams.
Points Rush: Teams play for Champion Rush Headstart Points across 6 matches on June 5th
Champion Rush: Teams play Champion Rush format on June 6th: Click here for details about Champion Rush format
