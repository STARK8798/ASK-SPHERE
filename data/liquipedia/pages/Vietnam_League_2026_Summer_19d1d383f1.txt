Vietnam League is a Free Fire league series in  Vietnam.


About


Format
* Registration from May 29th - June 3rd, 2026
Qualifiers: June 6th - 7th, 2026
Top 13 teams overall advance to Group Stage.

Group Stage: June 12th - 21st, 2026
18 teams:
5 teams from  FFWS SEA 2026 Spring.
13 teams from Qualifiers.
Week 1:
Teams divided into 3 groups of 6.
18 matches in total across 3 matchdays.
Teams play in a round-robin format.
Each team play total of 12 matches.
Based on the teams' final placements, they are divided into Top 12, 13th-15th, and 16th-18th pools for Week 2.
Week 2:
In Day 1, the Top 12 pool play and Top 3 teams advance to Grand Finals.
In Day 2, the remaining teams + 13th-15th of Week 1 pool play and Top 3 teams advance to Grand Finals.
In Day 3, the remaining teams + 16th-18th of Week 1 pool play and Top 6 teams advance to Grand Finals.

Grand Finals: June 27th - 28th, 2026
12 teams.
Points Rush: Teams play for Champion Rush Headstart Points across 6 matches on June 27th.
Champion Rush: Teams play Champion Rush format on June 28th: Click here for details about Champion Rush format
