Free Fire World Series - Southeast Asia (abbreviated as FFWS SEA) is the official regional league for  Southeast Asia.
The 2025 Pre-Season event acts as warm-up matches in adapting to the new 2025 esports format before the official 2025 Spring season with a total prizepool of $50,000 USD shared across the Battle Royale and Clash Squad modes.
