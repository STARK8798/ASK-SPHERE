Format
Knockout Stage: August 28th - September 13th, 2026
18 teams.
Week 1: August 28th - 30th, 2026
Teams are divided into 3 groups.
18 matches in total across 3 matchdays.
Teams play in a round-robin format.
Each team plays total of 12 matches.
Based on the teams' final placements, they are divided into 4 pools for Week 2.
Pool A - Top 12
Pool B - 13th - 14th
Pool C - 15th - 16th
Pool D - 17th - 18th
Week 2 Day 1: September 4th, 2026
The entirety of Pool A teams will play.
Top 2 teams advance to Grand Finals.
Remaining teams advance to Week 2 Day 2.
Week 2 Day 2: September 5th, 2026
Remaining 10 teams from previous day + 2 teams from Pool B teams will play.
Top 2 teams advance to Grand Finals.
Remaining teams advance to Week 2 Day 3.
Week 2 Day 3: September 6th, 2026
Remaining 10 teams from previous day + 2 teams from Pool C teams will play.
Top 2 teams advance to Grand Finals.
Remaining teams advance to Week 3.
Week 3: September 11th - 13th, 2026
Remaining 10 teams + 2 teams from Pool D
18 matches across 3 days.
Top 6 teams advance to Grand Finals.
